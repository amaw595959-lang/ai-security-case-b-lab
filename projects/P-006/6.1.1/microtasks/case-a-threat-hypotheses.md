# Case A threat hypotheses

## MT09 — Case A — Adversarial threat hypotheses

In the recorded Case B runs, an ordinary request led to a wrong or incomplete model answer. Case A asks what might happen if someone deliberately tried to cause a similar result. A wrong draft, an employee-ready preview and a copy in an operational sink are three separate outcomes. The canonical inventory names the risks below. Their connections to this notice workflow are proposed hypotheses, each with an untested adversarial outcome. Prior benign Case B evaluations and lab control checks keep their original verdicts. **How to verify and use it.** For each row, the learner should show the canonical risk name, where the hypothesized actor would control data, and the extra condition needed for an effect beyond private chat. Then describe a permitted synthetic check of the control and identify who could confirm the effect independently. A consultant uses these hypotheses to agree observation points and what evidence would settle a claim. This design accepts each row when it states the prerequisites, impact, expected control and verifiable check. The lab owner and designated independent reviewer would approve the exact future scope and evidence policy. No Case A test has been run for this supplement.
