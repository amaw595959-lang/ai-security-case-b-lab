# Security objectives and business impact

## MT10 — Security objectives and business impact

An objective describes an outcome the service desk needs. Requirements make the outcome checkable. For instance, a notice with an invented time may be useless even if it omits the internal ticket. The baseline linked broad objectives to requirements. This table expands the seven requested qualities with measurable evidence, possible business consequences and proposed responsible roles. The impacts are hypothetical for this fictional workflow. **How to verify and use it.** The learner should select an objective, explain whose work would be affected, identify the asset and role, and point to the relevant acceptance condition and evidence. In particular, explain what a successful healthy path can establish about availability and which unavailable-prerequisite or time-target claims still need evidence. A consultant uses the table to agree acceptance with workflow, platform, identity, data and evidence owners before assessment. The documented objectives and scoped observations meet documentary acceptance. Formal thresholds, applicability of organizational policy and proposed role assignments still need approval.
