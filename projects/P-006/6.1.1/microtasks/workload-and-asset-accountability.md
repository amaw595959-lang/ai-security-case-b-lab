# Workload and asset accountability

## MT04 — AI agents, service accounts and workload identities

The analyst authenticates as a person. The backend and model service are processes. They have their own permissions even when acting on the analyst's request. The model can suggest text, and authority remains with the backend and approved human records. The original inventory identifies the potential credentials. This register adds the custodian, least permissions and evidence needed to hold each service accountable. Boundary numbers refer to the existing architecture. [Workload inventory](../identities-and-assets.md#workload-identities) · [Data-flow diagram](../trust-boundaries.md#architecture)

Roles named as custodians are proposed. The specific people, workload principals and broader deployment grants remain `UNKNOWN`. Any credentials are inspected through sanitized configuration or approved secret references. Record an identifier and the authority it grants without retaining the secret value.
