# Micro-task coverage before and after

The before column records what was present at `9475f31459bc7bc89d51fdf10c518e995f0701e8`. Phase C is separately cited at its reviewed branch revision. The after column measures the supplementary documents in this directory. [Reviewed revisions](README.md#reviewed-revisions)

Count one of the 14 requested micro-tasks as `COVERED` when a reviewer can locate its objective, baseline evidence, applicable controls or limits, a named role or ownership gap, and a checkable acceptance criterion. A documented runtime or approval dependency can satisfy the requirement to record a gap. `PARTIAL` means the baseline addresses some of the task but lacks a requested artifact or linkage. `MISSING` means the baseline lacks the deliverable. `NOT_APPLICABLE` means the task is outside the agreed scope. All 14 tasks apply to this design review. These counts measure documentation only.
