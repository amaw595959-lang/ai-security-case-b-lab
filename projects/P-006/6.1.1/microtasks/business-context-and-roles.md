# Business context and human responsibilities

## MT01 — Enterprise business context and scope

The fictional service desk needs a timely and accurate maintenance notice. The analyst uses AI to draft it from approved service facts while keeping the internal ticket within the service desk. Employees need the service, time window including its clock, and action to take. A preview helps a second person review the content. The deliverable is a draft or an in-lab preview. Actual delivery requires its own authority and lies outside this engagement. The repository's scope and fixture already defined the use case. This supplement records the business decisions a sponsor or lab owner would need to verify. | --- | --- | --- |
| Content and approval | The analyst prepares the notice. Under the proposed workflow, the analyst confirms the intended audience and the assigned reviewer approves the service facts for the current revision and policy. | A service-desk workflow owner would own the audience and field policy. The synthetic fixture and Phase B's fixed reviewer assignment provide lab precedents. A real roster and policy approver are unknown. Phase B covers specific local routes and stores. General application and production destinations are unknown. **How to verify and use it.** The learner should explain in their own words who benefits, which fields each audience needs, and at which boundary the workflow stops. Walk one ambiguous and one explicit request through boundaries 1–5 and 8. Check B-04 against REQ-01 and B-05 against REQ-02, and locate REQ-05 through REQ-08 and INV-03 through INV-06 where the design limits destinations, effects, preview and copies. The original scope plus the decision and dependency table meet documentary acceptance when each inclusion, exclusion and approval has an evidence source or an explicit owner gap. A consultant uses this record to agree the engagement with the lab owner and a designated workflow sponsor before authorizing activity. Sponsor assignment and adoption of the proposed business decisions remain pending. [Review decisions](review-and-acceptance.md#decisions-still-open)

## MT03 — Human users, roles and responsibilities

A job in the notice workflow describes why a person is involved. An application role and resource grant describe what that account may actually do. The baseline identified standard, administrator and pending accounts and described the proposed analyst and audience. Phase B adds evidence of synthetic analyst and reviewer accounts. The matrix below adds responsibilities and handoffs.
