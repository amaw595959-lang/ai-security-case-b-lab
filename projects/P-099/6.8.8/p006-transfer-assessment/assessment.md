# Independent assessment record

This exercise prepares the client-style reporting and independent technical defense in work package 6.8.8. Its transfer scope is an isolated single application with fictional data. [Curriculum](../../../../strategy/334-subsublayers.md) · [Candidate brief](candidate.md)

## Scoring and required evidence

Award points against the recorded submission and a repeatable observation. Each criterion earns five points when its evidence is complete, two points when partial evidence supports a bounded claim, and zero when evidence is absent or contradicts the claim.

| Area | Points | Five-point criteria |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Scope and architecture | 15 | Authorized pinned deployment. Effective identities and asset classification. Correct trust boundaries and destinations. |
| Authorization and state | 20 | Verified distinct roles. Owner and venue permissions. Reviewer approval tied to revision. Denial with independent unchanged state. |
| Model evaluation | 15 | Oracle fixed before execution. Complete and traceable model trial records. Verdicts separated from backend effects. |
| Containment and data handling | 20 | Ready output from approved facts. Allowed metadata arrival. Excluded content checked at the monitored sink. Retention and access independently demonstrated. |
| Reproducibility and reporting | 20 | Commands, versions and bindings. Raw receipts and digest verification. Findings scoped to facts. Remediation and retest or explicit coverage gap. |
| Technical defense | 10 | Learner independently reproduces or explains the assessor-selected evidence. Learner defends limits and identifies missing evidence. |

The reviewer checks for fabricated records, unapproved scope and unsupported claims before scoring. Missing scope or evidence makes the affected criterion blocked or unverified. Points describe the observed work. Independent competence requires human review and demonstration.

Use `PASS` for a verified acceptance criterion and `FAIL` for a verified violation. Use `BLOCKED` when a prerequisite prevents execution, `INCONCLUSIVE` when an attempt lacks the evidence needed to decide, and `NOT_TESTED` for an unexercised path. Preserve the original evidence and the reason for each status.

## Review procedure

1. Capture the candidate's immutable submission and independently verify its checksums. Record the date, assessor and authorized reproduction target.
2. Establish the candidate's independent working conditions through the human assessment process. Ask the learner to reproduce and explain selected steps. Separately run applicable sealed checks against the authorized app after submission.
3. Record the observed commands, source changes, results and remediation or retest for each graded criterion. Preserve evidence gaps and the previous submission.
4. Keep AI-assisted comments separate from the human assessor's observations and decision. AI comments may identify inconsistencies or propose questions after submission. Independent competence depends on the learner's demonstrated work and the human review.

The sealed case selections and criteria are kept separately from the candidate-facing materials. Verify the recorded commitment before opening the evaluator packet for an authorized assessment. [Commitment](review-commitment.json)
